The Queen's Riddles

“Off with her head!” shouted the Queen of Hearts, condemning Alice for defying her rule.

But the King intervened. “Spare her life if she can solve your riddles.”

The Queen agreed, confident that Alice would fail. As Alice ventured through Wonderland, she discovered riddles, hidden secrets, and rules that weren’t always what they seemed. The White Rabbit offered clues, the Mad Hatter sold hints, and the Cheshire Cat watched with a mysterious grin.

But the Queen had a trick of her own: she began corrupting the riddles to ensure Alice’s failure.

Now, Alice must outsmart the Queen, uncover the truth, and earn her freedom.

Solve the riddles. Question the rules. Survive Wonderland.
